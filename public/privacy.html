Karachi Runner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Karachi Runner ("the game") is a free endless-runner game. This policy explains what information the game uses. Application ID: pk.karachi.runner.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit or sell personal information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We do not know who you are.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress (level, coins, unlocked characters, settings, high score and the name you choose for your character) in your device's local storage. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Third-party services

The game loads fonts from Google Fonts over HTTPS. Google may receive your IP address and device information as part of that request, under Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
