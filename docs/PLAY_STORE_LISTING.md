# Google Play listing draft – pk.karachi.runner

**Title (max 30):** Karachi Runner

**Short description (max 80):** Dash through Karachi, dodge traffic, take turns and chase the high score.

**Full description:**
Run through the streets of Karachi in a fast 3-lane endless runner.

- Switch lanes, jump and slide past buses, rickshaws, potholes and water tankers
- Turn left and right at junctions and choose between flyover and underpass forks
- Collect rupee coins and power-ups, and keep your hearts up
- Run past landmark buildings across Karachi's districts
- Pick your pace: Easy (no turns), Standard or Hard (frequent chained turns)
- Unlock new characters as you level up

Free to play. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases.
